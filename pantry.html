Starter kit

The 3 things to buy first

Tool

Granite mortar & pestle

Non-negotiable for curry paste and som tum. Wood or ceramic versions crack under the pounding — granite is the only material that holds up.

Check price →
Pantry

Fish sauce — Three Crabs or Megachef

These are the two brands we actually keep stocked at home. Three Crabs (Viet Huong) has a rounder, more balanced flavor than most grocery-store brands — not too sweet, not overly harsh. Megachef is pricier and fermented longer, which shows: it's smoother and less sharp straight out of the bottle, closer to a condiment than a cooking ingredient. Both cost more than the bottom-shelf options — worth it for how much it changes the final dish.

Pantry

Tamarind concentrate

The sour backbone of Pad Thai and several curries — not the same as tamarind candy or paste-with-seeds.

The upgrade tier

Tool

Carbon steel wok

Gets hotter than any nonstick pan, which is what stir-fried noodles and high-heat curries need to avoid steaming into mush.

Pantry

Palm sugar

Rounder and less sharp than white sugar — the sweetness that balances fish sauce and lime in most dishes.
