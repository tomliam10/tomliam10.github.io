About

Why Krok & Wok Exists

A small site built around one idea: Thai food deserves better explanations than most of the internet gives it.

The short version

Krok & Wok started as a way to actually learn Thai cooking properly — not by skimming a dozen half-finished blog posts, but by testing recipes until they were right, and writing down what actually mattered: the tamarind ratio, the wok temperature, the order things hit the pan.

Every recipe here has been cooked more than once before it's published. If something doesn't work, it doesn't go up — no filler, no recipes copied from somewhere else and lightly reworded.

What you'll find here

  • Recipes — tested at home, with the reasoning behind each step, not just a list of instructions.
  • The Thai food primer — history, regional differences, and culture, for anyone who wants the full picture, not just a recipe card.
  • City guides — real restaurant picks, sourced from reviews and local press, for when you'd rather eat out.
  • The Pantry — honest notes on which tools and ingredients are actually worth buying.

A work in progress

This site is early and growing — new recipes and guides go up roughly every couple of weeks. If there's a dish or a city you'd like covered, reach out through the contact page.
